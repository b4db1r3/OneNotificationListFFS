# OneNotificationListFFS

iOS loves hiding your notifications the moment you look away. This tweak tells it to stop.

**OneNotificationListFFS** keeps your Lock Screen notifications in one visible list, even after you unlock, relock, respring, or come back later. Instead of pushing older alerts into a separate hidden history that you have to swipe up to find, your notifications stay right on the Lock Screen where they are actually useful.

It keeps the experience feeling native, just less annoying: regular notifications, grouped notifications, and the Lock Screen list all stay familiar, but the disappearing act is gone.

## Features

- Keeps Lock Screen notifications visible after relock, until you tap them like a responsible adult.
- Stops notifications from getting buried in hidden history, so no more swipe-up treasure hunts.
- Keeps everything in one clean notification list, which unfortunately means you can no longer pretend you "didn't see it."
- Works with regular and grouped notifications.
- Includes a simple Settings toggle.
- No respring required when toggling the tweak.
